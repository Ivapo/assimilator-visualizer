**How Control-click arrives on macOS**, read at the `Cargo.lock` versions. winit 0.30.13

AppKit's own; its context-menu path goes through `menuForEvent:`, and SDL and GLFW on macOS
receive a Control-click as a left button with Control (right-click emulation is opt-in
there), so the binding is Left + Ctrl. Were it re-sent as a secondary click, right-drag
would already orbit, so nothing mis-fires either way; gate 13 confirms it on the trackpad.
Option + left-drag, the fallback, is not needed.

  far 1000, camera at 500 m.
  - Box tops span 1.55–2.64 m, which is view depth 497.36–498.45 m and NDC depth
    0.50155–0.50264.

    1e-5 in depth, about 168 steps.
